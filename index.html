01 Live from your car

Every number, straight from the car.

Model 3 and Model Y have no screen behind the wheel. Mount your phone there and Veltor shows what the car is doing right now, read from the car itself, not guessed from GPS.

  • Speed & gear
  • Power
  • Battery & range
  • Efficiency

  • Regen

02 Dashboards

Pick a look. Swap it any time.

Each dashboard is its own design with the same live data inside. Flick between them at a stop with ‹ ▦ ›, or open the list and jump straight to one.

    03 Engine sound

    Give it a voice.

    Engine sounds follow your speed and how hard you press. In the Sound tab you pick the sound, a gearbox (single-speed, automatic or paddles) and a character: calm GT or full Race.

    Hear the sounds in your browser →

    04 Charging

    Plug in. See it fill.

    When the car is charging, the dashboard turns into a charging screen: how fast, how long to your limit, how much energy has gone in, and the range it has added. Every charge also teaches Veltor your battery's real usable capacity, measured live, not assumed from the spec sheet.

    • Charge speed
    • Time to limit
    • Energy added
    • AC or DC fast
    05 Driving stats

    Every drive, remembered.

    Veltor logs every drive on your phone, by day, week, month and year. With Pro you also get real efficiency, phantom drain, what each drive cost and your battery's health.

    • Drives
    • Miles per day
    • Real mi/kWh · Pro
    • Energy cost · Pro
    • Phantom drain · Pro
    • Battery health · Pro
    06 Set up

    Set up once. Then just drive.

    1. 1Choose your carTesla for full live data, or any other car for GPS speed and engine sound.
    2. 2Add Veltor as a keyHold your key card on the reader when the app asks. The car approves it, not us.
    3. 3You're pairedMount the phone and go. Nothing passes through a server.

Support

Questions, bugs, and getting Veltor talking to your car

Need a hand? Email veltorapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. If something's broken, please include the diagnostics (see below) — it's the fastest route to a fix.

Getting started

  • Open Veltor and choose Tesla.
  • Enter your VIN. You'll find it in the Tesla app under Vehicle → About, or on the driver-side door jamb.
  • Stand next to your car with your key card and tap Connect.
  • When the car's screen asks to add a key, hold the card flat on the console reader until it beeps. Veltor connects on its own.

No car to hand? Veltor has a full demo drive — run the engine sound, rev counter and dashboard from a slider with no vehicle at all.

Pairing isn't working

  • Make sure Bluetooth is on and you're standing next to the car.
  • Check the VIN is exactly right — it's how Veltor recognises your car over Bluetooth.
  • Keep the key card flat on the reader until the car beeps; a brief tap often isn't enough.
  • If it still won't pair, toggle your phone's Bluetooth off and on, then try once more.
  • Every Tesla model and firmware is a little different. If yours won't pair, please send diagnostics — that's exactly what they're for.

Sending diagnostics

In the app go to Settings → Share diagnostics (there's also a button on the pairing error screen). This produces a short technical log of what happened during the connection.

Your VIN is removed automatically before the log can be shared, and nothing is ever sent anywhere unless you choose to send it.

Is Veltor safe for my car?

Yes. Veltor adds itself as a read-only key — it can read telemetry, and it cannot unlock, start, drive, or send any command to your vehicle. You can remove its key at any time from the car's touchscreen under Controls → Locks.

Privacy

Veltor has no servers and no accounts, and collects nothing. Your VIN never leaves your phone. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

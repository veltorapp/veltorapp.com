Veltor Sound

Engine sound, from your car's own browser.

Your speed drives the engine note, and it plays through the car's speakers. Nothing to install, no account, and nothing you do here leaves the car.

Set it up parked, then just drive — there is nothing to touch once it is running. The speed shown is GPS, not your car's speedometer. Three sounds are free here; the rest unlock with a code from the Veltor app, which also reads your car directly over Bluetooth for live dashboards and a launch timer.

Unlock every sound

In the Veltor app on your phone, open the Sound tab and scroll to Web unlock code. Type it here. Codes change every hour; once unlocked, this browser stays unlocked.

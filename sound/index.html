Veltor Sound

Engine sound, from your car's own browser.

Your speed drives the engine note, and it plays through the car's speakers. Nothing to install, no account, and nothing you do here leaves the car.

Set it up parked, then just drive — there is nothing to touch once it is running. The speed shown is GPS, not your car's speedometer. These are the free sounds. The Veltor app reads your car directly over Bluetooth for the full set, live dashboards and a launch timer.

Unlock every sound

Open the Veltor app on your phone: Engine Sound → Web unlock code. Type the code here. Codes change every hour; this browser stays unlocked.

The team

Two people, one obsession.

Veltor is built by a small, independent team. We design it, write it and test it on our own drives, and when you email us, one of us writes back.

Founder · CEO · Developer

Ria Mano

BSc, MSc
“Tesla gave the car its technology. I wanted to give it a soul.”

I've always admired Tesla for the engineering: the instant torque, and software that keeps getting better while the car sits on the drive. But the more I drove one, the more I felt something was missing. A car this quick should sound like it, and it should show you what it's doing.

Veltor is my answer. It reads your Tesla live over Bluetooth, as a read-only key, and turns what it hears into an engine note that rises and falls with the way you drive, and a dashboard worth looking at. It's the last piece of the puzzle I wanted for my own car.

Getting here took more sleepless nights than I can count, and I hold myself to one rule: I don't release anything I wouldn't use myself. I use Veltor every single day, and it's now on its way to the App Store and Google Play.

Our goal is to put Veltor in front of every Tesla owner who'd enjoy it, listen to what they tell us, and keep making it better. Give it a try. I think you'll love it, and if there's anything you'd change, I want to hear it.

Chief Marketing Officer · Lead Tester

Dr Mano

BTech, ME, PhD
“If it isn't right on my commute, it isn't ready for yours.”

I have two jobs at Veltor: finding the drivers who help shape the app, and making sure every build is right before it reaches them.

I recruited our beta testers from Tesla owners' groups on Facebook: real drivers, on iPhone and Android, across different Tesla models. The logs and feedback they send have shaped nearly every release, from how reliably the app connects to how the dashboards read at a glance.

Every test build comes with me on my commute, to work and home again, every day. If the sound drops out, a number reads wrong or the connection hesitates, I want to find it before you do.

Off the clock I'm usually on two wheels, so I know how much a good engine note matters. If you'd like to help test Veltor, or you run a Tesla owners' group, I'd love to hear from you.
